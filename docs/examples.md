# Examples

All examples live in `cmd/examples` and play live through PortAudio until you press Ctrl+C.
Run them from the repository root (they load sounds from `assets/`):

```
go run ./cmd/examples/<name>
```

Most have a commented out `p.ToSoundFile(...)` call: uncomment it (and comment out
`p.Listen()`) to render to a file instead.

| Example | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `showcase` | "Aurora", the showcase: a slow orchestral piece in D minor (about 4 minutes) using every synth and effect, no drums. Wavetable violins with chorus and an LFO on the position, subtractive celli, FM pizzicato bass, harp (into freeverb and a granulator that freezes in the interlude), flute with a pitch shifted piccolo shadow, horn solo, celesta (pingpong delay into shimmer), DX horns and a flangered bell pad, speech vocoding a wavetable pad (the mixer lines everything up with the vocoder), a grains choir and pulsar choir and soprano in a convolved church, pulsar raindrops, a wavetable drone on a generated table, and the modelled grand piano (rolled chords at dawn, weight under the chorale, the last cadence, its sustain pedal automated at every bar line). Everything plays through the `mixer`: the strings (with the dawn and dusk ladder filter) and the choir (in the convolved church) are groups, the FDN hall and the shimmer sky are effect buses every instrument sends to in its own amount, and a lookahead limiter guards the master. Prelude, theme, chorale, interlude, climax and coda, written with the `arrange`, `score` and `harmony` packages and played with a human feel (looser ensembles, breathing soloists, rolled bell pad chords) (every part is checked against the chords at the start, the harp arpeggios come from the arpeggiator) (the melodies in score notation, the filter and granulator automated per section), the schedule is printed while it plays. `-render aurora.wav -stems -seed 7` renders it offline with a stem per mixer strip, `-profile` reports the time every module takes |
| `creation` | "Creation": the seven days of Genesis 1 and 2, to the glory of God and of Jesus Christ, the Word through whom all things were made (John 1:1-3). Every day is an evening and a morning of six bars each, the seventh day 21 bars of rest: the Doxology (the Old Hundredth) and a plagal Amen, the figure of the cross in the horn leading into the last chord (Colossians 1:16, 20). Three beats to the bar (the Trinity) in E flat major, tuned in just intonation (pure ratios, only the chords that sound pure in it; `-tuning equal` for equal temperament, `-a4` for the reference pitch). Day 1: an open fifth (without form and void), the Spirit upon the waters, John 1:1 spoken through the `vocoder`, then the darkness (a ladder lowpass) lifts at "Let there be light". Day 2: the waters divided (strings very high and very low). Day 3: the land (timpani, bass, waves) and the plants in a canon after their kind (flute, oboe, clarinet). Day 4: moon, stars (celesta, bells) and sunrise (horns, choir). Day 5: the creatures of the sea and the birds (flute and piccolo birdsong, recordings of the sea and birds). Day 6: the beasts, then man in the image of God (the soprano and the horn, the piano). Recordings of creation (waves, birds, gulls, animals, children) fade in on their days, the choir and organ sing in a convolved church, the verse of every day is printed as it begins (KJV), every part is checked against the chords. `-render creation.wav -stems -seed 7` renders it offline |
| `subtractive` | Acid house jam: two 303 style lines with accents and slides (`!` and `~` step notation), internal and external LFOs on the cutoff, four on the floor drums |
| `subtractive2` | Guided tour of every feature of the subtractive synth and its presets, section by section (the schedule is printed at start) |
| `fm` | FM synth: a jam (electric piano chords with random velocities, FM bass, sparse bells) followed by a tour of all FM presets, each showing settings that change note by note (velocity, operator ratios and levels, mod depth, feedback, all 8 algorithms, an LFO on the mod depth), then a parade of the General MIDI inspired presets, two bars each with a phrase that suits the instrument. The schedule is printed at start |
| `dx` | Plays every voice of a DX7 style SysEx bank, two bars each: `go run ./cmd/examples/dx path/to/bank.syx` (without a file it plays the built in patches) |
| `granulator` | Electric piano, mallets and drums through the granulator, changing scene every 4 bars: a cloud, an octave shimmer, a freeze, a reverse smear, a stutter and a feedback cascade |
| `grains` | Tour of the granular sampler on recordings from assets: choir pad, frozen drone with an LFO on the position, glass keys with rising velocity, sea texture with rising density, vocal stutter, shimmer, scan speeds and a plucked arpeggio |
| `pulsar` | Tour of the pulsar synth: a vowel lead changing vowels per note, a formant pad swept by an LFO, a buzz bass with rising duty, one melody through all four windows, pulses rising from a rhythm into a tone, crackles with rising masking and laser chirps, then a parade of the other presets (choir, strings, brass, mallet, sub bass, wobble bass, insects, raindrops, motor), two bars each. The schedule is printed at start |
| `wavetable` | Tour of the wavetable synth on the tables in `assets/wavetables2048`: a pad drifting through DN Harm, a position sweep through OPFoldFMFB, a lead changing position per note, plucks with a growing position envelope, a bass with an opening filter, a growl with a speeding position LFO, bell keys, an anthem growing from 1 to 7 unison oscillators, an LFO on value input 1 scanning FMAdditive1 and drum hits at random positions, then a parade of the other presets (strings, vox, brass, organ, reese, acid, stab, drone, glass, riser), two bars each on a table that suits them. The schedule is printed at start |
| `piano` | Tour of the physically modelled piano: the grand plays a nocturne in E flat (an arpeggiated left hand, the sustain pedal changed at every bar line by an automation lane, the strings ringing along), then one chord from pianissimo to fortissimo, an arpeggio over the whole keyboard, short chords dry, at half pedal and with the pedal down, a low C caught by the sostenuto under dry chords, and a phrase with and without the soft pedal; then the other presets: bright pop chords, an upright hymn, a honky tonk boogie (swung), felt piano arpeggios and a toy piano music box, and a parade of the concert, warm, baby, vintage and cinematic presets playing the same phrase. Tempo per section, all pianos on a mixer with a hall (the Musikverein in Vienna: a recorded impulse response through the `convolver`). `-render piano.wav -seed 3` renders a pass |
| `keyboard` | Play the synths live from a MIDI keyboard, in the Musikverein: the piano, subtractive, pulsar, wavetable, fm, dx and grains with all their presets. A raw MIDI device (`-device`, `-list` shows them), or JACK audio with a JACK MIDI port (`-jack`). Choose the synth and preset (`-synth`, `-preset`) and the velocity curve (`-curve soft`, `linear`, `hard` or an exponent). Bank select chooses the synth and a program change its preset, or type a synth or preset name in the terminal (`list` shows them). The piano's pedals work as on a piano (sustain with half pedaling, sostenuto, soft), the sustain pedal holds the notes of the other synths, `-monitor` prints every event and `-demo` plays a phrase as MIDI without a keyboard. See [MIDI](midi.md) |
| `wavegen` | Tour of the generated wavetables: a brightness lead, PWM organ chords, a sync lead, FM bells, a wavefolded bass, a voice singing vowels, opening drawbars and a pad through random spectra (4 bars each), then two bars each of a resonant filter sweep, CZ resonance, odd and even harmonics, a comb, saturation, bit reduction, steps, a shape crossfade and tables sliced from a choir and a vocal recording, the position rising note by note. `go run ./cmd/examples/wavegen tables` also saves the tables as WAV files in `tables` |
| `groove` | Feel: one jazz loop (a ii V I VI with voice led comping and a walking bass, written with `harmony`) played four ways, changing the feel per section: on the grid, swung 8ths, a 16th shuffle, and swung with human timing, dynamics, accented hihats, a strummed electric piano and a laid back bass |
| `generative` | "Weather", a generative piece: no written notes, generator driven `arrange` streams play the parts (pad chords from a shuffle bag of the key's chords with voice leading, a pulsar line wandering through D dorian in random rhythms, a tresillo bass on the chord root) and every section is a scene whose parameters reach the stream templates (formant, cutoff) and the granulator (mix, freeze, octave) with `SendParams`, while streams also send the granulator new grain settings (messages) and wander the pan of the glass strip (values). Different every loop, repeatable with `-seed`, `-render` renders a pass |
| `filters` | Tour of the filter modules: SVF, ladder lowpass and bandpass, biquad peak EQ wah and formant vowel morph, each swept by an LFO |
| `vocoder` | Speech vocoding a chord pad, with a flute melody, a pad, falling stars and panning, using `latency` to line everything up |
| `modulation` | Tour of the chorus and the flanger in A minor, four bars per scene: two bars dry, then two with the effect, so you hear what it adds (the settings come per section, an automation lane brings in the mix). Chorus: a subtle electric piano chorus, lush strings, the spread swept from mono to wide, a doubler and a resonant chorus with feedback and a darker tone. Flanger: a tempo synced jet on drums, negative feedback on a saw pad, a stereo swirl and the manual delay swept by automation. Both effects are inserts on `mixer` groups, a dry bass plays throughout. `-render modulation.wav -seed 3` renders a pass |
| `reverb` | Plucks and a pad through the FDN reverb (large dark hall) |
| `shimmer` | Slow notes through the shimmer reverb, rising an octave per pass |
| `convolution` | Plucks through the convolver with a recorded church impulse response (or a synthetic one) |
| `drums`, `drums2` | Sample based drum patterns with the `drums` synth, steps and templates (`drums2` also arranges parts over time: every part on a `mixer` strip, muted and unmuted per section with `arrange` and `Play`) |
| `sampler` | Speech regions played by a `player` with flanger, pingpong delay and freeverb |
| `granular/drops`, `granular/heavenly`, `granular/loopup` | Granular synthesis with custom parameter generators, sources and envelopes |
